# Project delivery closeout | P-NNNN

- Scope baseline, final delivered revision or outcome, closure recommendation and date
- Planned versus actual scope, cost, milestones and outcomes, with evidence and unresolved variance
- Linked acceptance, release or delivery evidence, final user and technical docs, and register status
- Handoff recipient and acceptance decision, support owner and backup when applicable, and runbook and maintenance record links
- Open defects, risks or obligations, assigned owner, due date and follow-up checkpoint
- Lessons and measurable follow-up improvements; benefits still to verify, owner and check date
- PM recommendation, named closure decision owner, dated decision and record links

Record delivery closure and service support status separately. For a live app, hand over ongoing maintenance to the named support owner. Formal transfer or approved retirement governs when that support responsibility ends.
