# Project charter | P-NNNN

- Project title, sponsor/CEO, Project Manager, owners, version, date and status
- Approved problem, desired outcome and observable success criteria
- Scope, exclusions and deliverables; requirements and acceptance evidence source
- Budget ceiling ($0 in new cash spend by default), currency, forecast period, approved allowances, key milestones, capacity assumptions and independently checked cost-plan link
- Project roles, escalation route, named business requirements decision owner and delegated limits, and how changes are routed
- For commercial engagements, restricted agreement reference, authorised signatory, milestone acceptance owner and evidence, billing owner, onboarding and support terms, and renewal or end-of-service link
- Material dependencies, risks and constraints, with owners and next checkpoints
- Selected ways of working, go-live and support expectations, and applicable specialist reviews
- Linked proposal, project record, current evidence, and exact CEO build decision and date

Keep the approved version identifiable. Link any subsequent change decision and revised baseline. Production approval is recorded separately for the release candidate.
