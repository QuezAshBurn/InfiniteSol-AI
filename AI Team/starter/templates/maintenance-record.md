# Maintenance record | P-NNNN / application name

- Application, project ID, supported environments and critical user workflows
- Current live revision, release decision, deployed date with zone, and smoke-check or live health evidence
- Named support owner, named backup, authorised production operator and access references
- Coverage hours and zone, approved response and recovery targets (if agreed), escalation route, acknowledgement method and fallback
- Runbook path and version, approved operator actions and limits, and rollback or restore procedure references
- Handoff status, accepting owner and date, release-gate evidence, and outstanding readiness gaps
- Post-deploy verification deadline with time zone, interim contact, blocked acceptance reason and actual escalation evidence when applicable
- Where monitoring and alerts are configured, alert delivery rehearsal reference, and actual coverage or limitations
- Backup, retention and last restore rehearsal evidence or recorded applicability decision
