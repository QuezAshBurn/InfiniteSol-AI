# Improvement and opportunity record | P-NNNN / OPP-NNN

- Proposal type, proposer, date with zone, and current disposition
- Original requirement or signal, exact source, affected user or buyer and observed problem
- Current baseline, source and evidence status; assumptions, contrary evidence and unknowns
- Options including status quo, likely impact and effort, support cost, risks, dependencies and what work would be displaced
- Test or experiment, owner, authorised time and spend limit, approval reference, success threshold and stop rule
- Observed result, window and sample, limitations, and recommendation
- Decision to investigate, with scope and authority; separate decision to implement, or reason to decline or defer
- Approved baseline version, backlog and acceptance criteria links if adopted; follow-up owner, due date and actual benefits where measured
