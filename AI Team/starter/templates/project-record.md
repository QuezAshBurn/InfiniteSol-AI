# Project record | P-NNNN

- Title and requested outcome
- Original request, requester and date
- Project owner, priority, stage and optional blocked or paused flag
- Trusted source locations and permitted tools, environments and data
- Scope, exclusions, budget ceiling and links to authoritative approvals
- Current repository baseline and candidate revisions, by component
- Review pack path, QA evidence paths, dependencies and next milestone
- Selected ways of working, accountable roles, next process review, study and exercise records
- Improvement and opportunity register, linked requirement baseline and decisions, with project-local evidence paths
- Project proposal, current approved charter and decision ID, document register at `docs/document-register.md`, and delivery closeout when available
- Prototype decision and evidence or rationale for skipping, architecture brief, relevant architecture decisions, project coding standard and review evidence
- Approved Markdown for the assignment, exact revisions, scope and decision references
- Deployment Engineer and authorised operator, runbook, GitHub and deployment runs, environment promotion and exact candidate evidence
- Development, Testing, Staging and Production readiness and verified support handoff

- Current open-source and cost plan, approved budget ceiling and period, Finance verification and any pending cost decision

- Data and BI contract, owners, metric decisions, reconciliation evidence and operational handoff where applicable

- Commercial handoff references and approved decision owners, milestone acceptance and invoice evidence, onboarding and support handoff, renewal or end-of-service owner, and readiness exercise results where applicable

## Release checklist

- Applicable business-readiness rehearsal and verified owners recorded against `workflows/BUSINESS-OPERATIONS.md`

- Identified integrated candidate and approved acceptance baseline
- Latest peer approval tied to the reviewed source revisions mapped to this release candidate, with required automated checks and independent QA evidence on the exact release candidate
- Passing Testing and Staging evidence linked to the same proposed production artifact, with configuration revisions, material parity gaps resolved, and verified rollout and rollback readiness
- Integration-originated content or conflict resolutions identified and independently reviewed under separate review IDs, or recorded as none
- Each change and build has technical documentation and evidence; the Technical Writer has verified every affected document for the exact candidate
- Applicable specialist dispositions; blockers corrected and verified
- Deployment and Finance recheck of the actual configuration and zero-charge controls against the current approved ceiling
- People and process change management plan with adoption readiness evidence where affected
- Current approved release and rollback runbook with backup or recovery evidence, rollback threshold and operational owner, linked in the document register
- Dated CEO approval tied to this candidate and named authorised release operator
- Actual outcome, post-release checks and post-release support owner
- Linked per-application maintenance record, verified alert and recovery readiness where applicable, and support-owner acceptance following deployment
