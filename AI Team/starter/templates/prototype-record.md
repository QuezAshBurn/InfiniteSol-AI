# Prototype record | Project and experiment ID

- Owner, dates, timebox and authorised scope, environment and data
- Decision to inform, requirement and risky assumption or hypothesis
- Method and prototype type, inputs, representative scenario and exclusions
- Success and stop criteria agreed before the experiment
- Actual observations, evidence links, check status and limits
- Decision to proceed, revise, repeat or stop, with evidence and dated decision reference
- Follow-up owners, affected requirements and architecture decisions, and disposition of experimental files
- Proposed reuse, if any, with an approved implementation task and normal design, review and QA links
