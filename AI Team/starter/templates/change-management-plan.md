# Change management plan | P-NNNN

- Intended change and why it matters, accountable adoption owner, version and date
- Affected groups, current and target workflows, impacts and dependencies
- Communication audience, draft message, sender, channel and timing, with required publication authority
- Training or job aids, responsible owner, dates and evidence of readiness
- Pilot or rollout approach and support, known objections or barriers, and staff feedback route
- Readiness checks, adoption baseline, success measure, checkpoint dates, and fallback if readiness is poor
- References to the approved scope, any change request, release decision and updated support documents

Report what was drafted, approved, sent, delivered or verified separately. Release approval remains in the project decision log.
