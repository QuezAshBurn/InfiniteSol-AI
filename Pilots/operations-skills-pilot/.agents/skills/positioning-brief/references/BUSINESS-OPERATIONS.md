# Customer commitments and business operations

Use the customer handoffs for commercial projects and the readiness checks for the first live release. Use the existing portfolio, project charter, project record and maintenance record. Keep customer contracts, billing details and personal information in their approved locations and link only the identifiers or restricted references needed for handoff. The PM tracks decisions and readiness. Each action requires the applicable approved Markdown and execution permission.

| Handoff | Accountable work and evidence |
|---|---|
| Lead to qualified opportunity | Product Marketing records the customer problem, fit, scope assumptions and evidence. The PM records the opportunity and routes the decision to proceed. External outreach requires its own permission. |
| Opportunity to signed engagement | PM coordinates the proposed scope, milestones, acceptance, cost and support terms. Legal routes the draft for any required qualified review, Finance verifies pricing and billing terms, and the named authorised signatory executes the agreement. PM records the signed agreement reference and the dated decision. The agreement is commercial evidence. The approved charter and work authorization must cover delivery before work begins. |
| Agreement to delivery and acceptance | PM links milestones, acceptance criteria and the business requirements decision owner to the approved project baseline. Record who can accept each milestone. The business decision owner settles requirements within delegated authority. Material scope, spend, priority and Production decisions go to the CEO. |
| Accepted milestone to billing | The named billing owner checks the agreed billing trigger against the agreement and acceptance evidence, records the invoice reference and due date, and reconciles payment status with Finance. Issuing invoices or taking payment requires the applicable human or system authorisation. Record disputes, owner and follow-up. |
| Customer onboarding and ongoing service | Marketing and the Technical Writer prepare approved customer materials. The PM confirms onboarding, acceptance, support hours, owner, backup, contact and escalation. Deployment hands off the verified release and runbook to the named support owner. Track renewal or end-of-service dates where applicable and route related commitments to the authorised decision maker. |

## Rehearse before committing or going live

Before the first paid customer commitment and first live release, run a synthetic commercial handoff and critical-incident exercise under approved Markdown. Refresh the affected rehearsal when owners, terms, service coverage, tools or workflows materially change. Follow `workflows/MAINTENANCE.md` for operational tests, ownership, recovery and release readiness.

1. Use fictitious inputs to route a qualified lead, approved terms, signed-reference placeholder, build authorization, accepted milestone, draft invoice, onboarding, support and renewal. Verify that a missing authorised signatory or agreement blocks the commitment, a missing build authorization blocks implementation, and missing contractual billing evidence blocks billing.
2. While one simulated build is active, inject a critical service incident and make the PM unavailable. Verify the backup can find the approved incident delegation, assess the threshold, preserve the build checkpoint, escalate and record who can restart the build. Test an absent or expired delegation and a request for new spending or an unapproved release. Retain the known outcome or block the dependent action. Use only approved simulated data and the authorised route for a live synthetic alert test.
3. Record the approved rehearsal scope, participants, timestamps, setup, expected and actual actions, evidence, pass or blocked status and corrective owners in the portfolio and linked project or support record. Validate actual contact acknowledgement, authorised access and owner availability with the named support owner, backup and Production operator before claiming operational readiness. A tabletop result establishes the workflow's behaviour on paper. Missing real evidence remains open and blocks the applicable customer commitment or release gate.
