# Work authorization | Project, phase and assignment ID

- Owner, revision, status, date and time zone
- Observable task or story goal and linked approved requirement or decision
- Measurable criteria, expected evidence per criterion, required approach or constraints and existing behaviour to preserve
- Allowed actions, exclusions, tools, allowed paths, environment and data
- Authoritative Markdown paths, exact revisions and approval references
- Responsible owner, independent QA owner, permitted time or spend, and stop or escalation conditions
- Required outputs and where to record decisions, changes and evidence
- Approver, decision, date, delegated authority if applicable, and expiration or review trigger
- If authorising a commercial or readiness action, identify the specific handoff, named executor, permitted contact or sandbox, and evidence required. Record synthetic rehearsal scope and any checks requiring actual named owners.
- For a critical-incident pause delegation, record CEO decision, trigger, delegate and backup, time limit, scope, notification route and restart authority.
- Prior revision or decision superseded, and affected checks to rerun where applicable
