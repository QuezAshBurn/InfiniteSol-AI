# Finance case packets

All cases use fictional records and AUD. Grants are current, read-only and limited to internal finance review. The variance convention is estimate at completion minus approved baseline.

## FIN-01
Approved cap 1000. Incurred invoices are 300. A 500 commitment includes 200 already counted in the 300, leaving 300 outstanding. Additional uncommitted forecast is 200. Exact records cover the approved period and quote rates. Documented downside adds 100. Model reports 800 expected, 900 downside and -200 base variance. The decision asks only whether the scoped model reconciles.

## FIN-02
Cap 1000, incurred 300. A 500 commitment includes 200 incurred. Additional uncommitted forecast 200. The submitted model adds the full committed amount to actuals and forecasts 1000. Evidence shows the overlap exactly.

## FIN-03
Production must meet approved AUD 0 in new spend. Current plan includes a hosted quota, but the console only confirms a usage alert. The plan documents automatic per-use billing beyond quota. Unchanged deployment is requested and no spending approval exists.

## FIN-04
Approved scope is Development and Testing only with AUD 0 new spend. Documented existing local capacity, permitted use, expiry and peak-volume exercise confirm no external billing path for that scope. Staff time and shared equipment are recorded separately. Staging and Production feasibility are unknown and outside the requested disposition.
